How should the pan control integrate with the existing SceneCard thumbnails?

A: Overlay Hint + Direct Drag

Hover shows a subtle "drag to reposition" overlay on the existing thumbnail. Drag directly on the thumbnail to pan. The refresh button moves to a small icon in the corner instead of being a full overlay.

Pros

  • Zero extra UI — reuses existing thumbnail
  • Feels native and immediate
  • Works identically across all 3 templates

Cons

  • Small drag surface (80×96px on slideshow)
  • Conflicts with existing hover-to-refresh overlay

B: Click-to-Expand Inline Preview

Click the thumbnail to expand it inline to ~2x size with a violet border. Drag in the larger preview to pan. Shows position coordinates. Click outside to collapse. Refresh button stays as a small corner icon.

Pros

  • Larger drag surface for precision
  • No conflict with refresh overlay
  • Position feedback visible

Cons

  • Extra click to enter/exit crop mode
  • Expanding thumbnail may shift layout

Pan button click:

C: Toolbar Buttons Below Thumbnail

Small icon buttons appear below each thumbnail: crop/pan, refresh, and reset. Clicking crop enters pan mode on the thumbnail. Explicit controls, discoverable, but adds vertical space.

Pros

  • Most discoverable — buttons are always visible
  • Clear separation of actions
  • Reset button for quick undo

Cons

  • More UI clutter on every scene card
  • Still small drag surface
  • Most users won't need crop on most images
